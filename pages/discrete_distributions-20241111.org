* Terms
- PMF :: Probability mass function is the probability distribution of a discrete random variable. $PMF_{X}(x)=P(X=x)$
- CDF :: $CDF_{X}(x)=P(X\le x)=\sum_{k\le x}PMF_X(k)$

* Skewed Data

- Positive Skewed == Right Skewed
- Negative Skewed == Left Skewed

* Examples
** Rolling the Dice (Uniform Distribution)
#+begin_src python
# Rolling the Dice
np.random.seed(0)

# PMF of a fair 6-sided die
die = np.array([1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6])
prob = np.ones(6) / 6
df = pd.DataFrame({'number': die, 'prob': prob})

expected_value = np.mean(df['number'])

# Plot the PMF
plt.bar(df['number'], df['prob'])
plt.show()

# Simulate rolling (Sampling)
rolls_10 = df.sample(100, replace=True)
rolls_10['number'].hist(bins=np.linspace(1,7,7))

# CDF of a fair 6-sided die
# CDF is the area under the PMF
cdf = np.cumsum(prob)
#+end_src
