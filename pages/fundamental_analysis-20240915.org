#+title: Fundamental Analysis
#+filetags: :FA:Trading:

* Definition
Evaluating a stock's price based on it's revenues, earnings, future growth, return on equity,
profit margins, etc.

Evaluation is done by examining a company's income statements, it's earning releases and balance sheet.

* Statements
** Balance Sheet
*** Market Cap
Market price times total shares
- Small: 300M ~ 2B
- Medium: 2B ~ 10B
- Large: 10B ~

*** Book Value
- Book Value = Tangible assets (sellable) - Liabilities
- Equity = Total assets - Liabilities

** Income Statement
- Net Income (Earnings) :: Revenue - Expenses
** Cash Flow Statement
** Example (Without Intangible Assets)
- IPO Balance Sheet $y_{0}$
|             | Company A | Company B |
|-------------+-----------+-----------|
| Shares Out  | =30M=     | =90M=     |
| IPO Price   | =$1.5=    | =$1.5=    |
| Liabilities | =$2M=     | =$5M=     |
| Equity      | =$45M=    | =$135M=   |
| Assets      | /$47M/    | /$140M/   |

- Income Statement $y_{1}$
|            | Company A | Company B |
|------------+-----------+-----------|
| Net Income | $2M       | $5M       |

- Cash Flow $y_{1}$
|           | Company A | Company B |
|-----------+-----------+-----------|
| Dividends | $2M       | $1M       |

- Balance Sheet $y_{1}$
|             | Company A     | Company B       |
|-------------+---------------+-----------------|
| Shares Out  | 30M           | 90M             |
| Liabilities | =$2M=         | =$5M=           |
| Equity      | /$45M=45+2-2/ | /$139M=135+5-1/ |
| Assets      | /$47M/        | /$144M/         |

- Market $y_{1}$
| Market Price | =$2=           | =$2.5=           |
| Market Cap   | /$2*30M=$60M/  | /$2.5*90M=$225M/ |
| Book Value   | /$47M-2M=$45M/ | /$144M-5M=$139M/ |
| BVPS         | /$1.5/         | /$1.54/           |
