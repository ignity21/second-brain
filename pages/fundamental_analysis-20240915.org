#+title: Fundamental Analysis
#+filetags: :FA:Trading:

* Definition
Evaluating a stock's price based on it's revenues, earnings, future growth, return on equity,
profit margins, etc.

Evaluation is done by examining a company's income statements, it's earning releases and balance sheet.
* Ratios
** Activity Ratios
- Turnover
- Total Assets Turnover
** Liquidity Ratios
** Solvency Ratios
Conveys information on the company's ability to pay long-term debt.
- Debt to Equity Ratio(Total Debt / Total Shareholder's equity)

** Profitability Ratios
- ROE (Net Income / Average Total Equity)
- EPS
- P/E
- EPS next Year
- Forward P/E

** Valuation Ratios

* Statements
** Balance Sheet
*** Market Cap
Market price times total shares
- Small: 300M ~ 2B
- Medium: 2B ~ 10B
- Large: 10B ~

*** Book Value
- Book Value = Tangible assets (sellable) - Liabilities
- Equity = Total assets - Liabilities

** Income Statement
- Net Income (Earnings) :: Revenue - Expenses
** Cash Flow Statement
** Fundamental Metrics
- Price: Price per share
- Book/sh: Book per share
- P/B: Price / (Book/sh)
- EPS: Earnings per share
- P/E: Price / EPS
- ROE: Return on investment(Net Income / Equity(t-1)); How much the company can make for each dollar of equity
