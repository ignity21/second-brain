* Load Balancer
A type of reverse proxy that distributes traffic cross servers.

* Load Balancing Strategies
- Round-robin
- Weighted Round-robin
- Random Selection
- Performance-based Selection: Load balancer performs health checks on servers.
- IP-based Routing: helps maximized cache hits. [[id:e3dd92d2-41be-468c-b7ff-323a63209773][Hashing]]
- Path-based Routing
