* Generalization
A model is able to make accurate predictions on unseen data.
- It is able to generalize from the training set to the test set.

** Generalization Error Causes
*** Bias
This part of the generalization error is due to *wrong assumptions*.
A high-bias model is most likely to underfit the training data.

*** Variance
This part is due to the model's excessive sensitivity to small
variations in the training data.

A model with *many degrees of freedom* (such as a high-degree polynomial model)
is likely to have high variance and thus overfit the training data.

- Solution: [[id:483b6d38-f2bc-4879-84ab-27e0a76561fa][Regularization]]

*** Irreducible Error
This part is due to the *noisiness of the data itself*. The only way to reduce this
part of the error is to clean up the data.

*** *Trade-off*
- Increasing a model's complexity will typically increase its variance and reduce its bias.
- Reducing a model's complexity increases its bias and reduces its variance.

** Generalization Performance
*** Cross-validation
- If a model performs well on the training data but generalizes poorly
  according to the cross-validation metrics, then your model is overfitting.
- If it performs poorly on both, then it is underfitting.

*** See [[id:807f0a93-ac07-434f-a71b-33e011a87f9e][Learning Curves]]
To generate the plots, train the model several times on different sized subsets of the training set.
- [[file:~/second-brain/notebooks/ML/plot_learning_curves.ipynb][Plotting Learning Curves]]

* Overfitting/Underfitting Causes
- Choosing too simple a model is called underfitting
- Overfitting occurs when you fit a model too closely to the particularities of the training set.
- If dataset is large enough, it becomes harder for a model to overfit, or memorize the data.
- Too many features will cause overfitting

* Fixing Underfitting
1. use a more complex model
2. come up with better features

* Reducing Overfitting
1. Reduce number of features
   - Manually select which features to keep.
   - [[id:929621f8-f5c6-4bb1-8ce7-c5c79fcdafa6][Model Selection]] Algorithm
2. Feed more training data until the *validation error* reaches the *training error*.
3. Regularization: Keep all the features, but reduce magnitude/values of parameters $\theta_j$
