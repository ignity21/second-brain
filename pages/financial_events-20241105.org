* Economic Cycle
- Expansion
- Peak
- Contraction
- Trough

** What Represents a Strong Economy
- Increase in economic output (GDP)
- Decrease in unemployment
- Increase in inflation
- Increase in investments (capital expenditure)
- *Political Stability*

* Government Policies
** [[id:d190f854-9f8c-4757-b14a-304c1f4d97f9][Monetary Policy]]
** Fiscal Policy
- Lower taxes -> More money in circulation, more spending
  - stimulate the economy
- Higher taxes -> Less money in circulation
  - Positive Impact: Use taxes to stimulate the economy
  - Negative Impact: Use taxes to pay back their debts

* Important Reports
** EIA Petroleum Status Report
- EIA (Energy Information Administration) Report
  - Weekly that comes out at (9:30/10:30) EST every wednesday
  - It provides weekly information on petroleum *inventory* changes
  - Inventory decreases -> more demand / less supply -> price up
  - See on [[https://www.bloomberg.com/markets/economic-calendar][Bloomberg Calendar]]

** Jobless Claims Report
- Weekly on [[https://www.bloomberg.com/markets/economic-calendar][Bloomberg Calendar]] (7:30/8:30) EST every Thursday

** Housing Market Index
- Monthly survey
  - Done by the members of the National Association of Home Builders
  - Measures demand and conditions in the single-family housing market
- If it increases(big demand), that's good for economy -> spending up (furniture/appliances market up)

** Consumer Sentiment Index
- Monthly survey
  - Done to at least 500 respondents that answer 50 questions concerning near-term consumption and spending attitude

* LEI(Leading Economic Indicators)
- Monthly(around 20th)
  - [[https://www.conference-board.org/topics/us-leading-indicators][For US]]
  - https://www.conference-board.org/data
- Composition of LEI index:
  - Unemployment claims
  - Stock prices
  - Orders of consumer goods
  - Plant and equipment orders
  - Length of workweek
  - Speed of deliveries
  - Money supply
  - Building permits
  - Yield spread
  - Consumer expectations

* CEI(Coincident Economic Indicators)
- Monthly(around 20th)
  - [[https://www.conference-board.org/topics/us-leading-indicators][For US]]
- Difference from LEI: It doesn't *lead* the economy.
- Composition of CEI index:
  - Average hours worked in manufacturing
  - Nonfarm payroll employment
  - Unemployment rate
  - Wages and salary
