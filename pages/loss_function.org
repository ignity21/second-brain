*** Binary Cross Entropy
\[L(f_{\bm{w}, b}(\bm{x^{(i)}}), y^{(i)})=
-\log(f_{\bm{w}, b}(\bm{x^{(i)}}))
-(1-y)\log(1-f_{\bm{w}, b}(\bm{x^{(i)}}))\]
