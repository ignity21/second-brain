* Definition
An *eigenvector* of a *square matrix* $M$ is a non-zero vector that remains on the same line after transformation by the linear transformation
\[M\cdot\bm{v}=\lambda \times \bm{v}\]
Where $\lambda$ is a scalar value called the eigenvalue associated to the vector $\bm{v}$.

* Finding Eigenvalues
\[M\cdot\bm{v}=\lambda \times (I\cdot \bm{v})\]
\[M\cdot\bm{v}-(\lambda\times I)\cdot \bm{v}=0\]
\[(M-\lambda\times I)\cdot \bm{v}=0\]
- $det((M-\lambda\times I))$ should be 0 to transform a non-zero $\bm{v}$ into $\vec{0}$
