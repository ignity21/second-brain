#+title: Binomial Distribution
#+filetags: :Math:Statistics:Probability:

* Independence
#+begin_quote
The binomial distribution is a probability distribution
of the number of successes in a sequence of *independent*
trials.
#+end_quote

* Python Examples
** Generating Data
#+begin_src python
from scipy.stats import binom

# Flip 1 coin with 50% chance of heads 10 times
data = binom.rvs(1, 0.5, size=10)

# Flip 10 coins with 50% chance of heads 2 times
binom.rvs(10, 0.5, size=2)
#+end_src

** Calculating PMF & PDF
#+begin_src python
from scipy.stats import binom

# binom.pmf(num heads, num trials, prob of heads)
# What's the prob of getting 3 heads in 5 coin flips?
p = binom.pmf(3, 5, 0.5)

# What's the prob of getting 3 or fewer heads in 5 coin flips?
p = binom.cdf(3, 5, 0.5)
#+end_src
