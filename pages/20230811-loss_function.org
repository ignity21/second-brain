\[MSE(f_{\bm{w}, b}(\bm{x}), y)=(f_{\bm{w}, b}(\bm{x})-y)^2\]

* Classification
** Binary Classification
*** Binary Cross Entropy
\[L(f_{\bm{w}, b}(\bm{x^{(i)}}), y^{(i)})=
-\log(f_{\bm{w}, b}(\bm{x^{(i)}}))
-(1-y)\log(1-f_{\bm{w}, b}(\bm{x^{(i)}}))\]
- ~tensorflow.keras.losses.BinaryCrossentropy()~

** Multi-class Classification
*** Sparse Categorical Cross Entropy
\[a_j=\frac{e^{z_j}}{\sum_{k=1}^N e^{z_k}}=P(y=j|\bm{x})\]
- note: \(a_1+a_2+\ldots+a_N=1\)

\[L(a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_N,y) = \begin{cases}
-log(a_1) & \quad \text{if } y=1 \\
-log(a_2) & \quad \text{if } y=2 \\
\vdots \\
-log(a_N) & \quad \text{if } y=N \\
\end{cases}\]
