#+title: Multivariate Chain Rule
#+filetags: :Calculus:Math:

$$\frac{df}{dt}=\frac{\partial f}{\partial \vec{x}}\frac{d\vec{x}}{dt}$$

$$\frac{df}{dt}=\frac{\partial f}{\partial \vec{x}}\frac{\partial \vec{x}}{\partial \vec{u}}\frac{d\vec{u}}{dt}$$
