** Factors
*** Float
- Low float
  - It doesn't require a lot of money to move it. It may suitable for momentum strategies.
  - However, it also increases risk due to potential for large swings in either direction.

*** Institutional Ownership
- High institutional ownership
  - It can suggest stability
  - Institutions are not likely to buy as stock is going up because institutions would like to buy a lot of shares.
