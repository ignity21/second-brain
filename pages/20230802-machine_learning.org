* Definition
A computer program is said to learn from experience /E/ with respect to some class
of tasks /T/ and performance measure /P/, if its performance at tasks in /T/, as
measured by /P/, improves with experience /E/.

- E = the experience of playing many games of checkers
- T = the task of playing checkers.
- P = the probability that the program will win the next game.

** Terminology
- Task :: Classifying emails as spam or not spam.
- Experience :: Watching you label emails as span or not spam.
- Performance Measure :: The number of emails correctly classified as spam/not spam.

*** Spam Filter Example
Your spam filter is a Machine Learning program that, given examples of spam emails
(e.g., flagged by users) and examples of regular emails, can learn to flag spam.

- The examples that the system uses to learn are called the *training set*.
- Each training example is called a *training instance* (or *sample*).
- The task *T* is to flag spam for new emails.
- The experience *E* is the training data.
- The performance measure *P* needs to be defined
  - For example, the ratio of correctly classified emails.
  - This performance measure is called accuracy, and it is often used in classification tasks.

* ML Terminology
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       e5d68507-2640-44b2-b5a3-01b63b663bfb
:END:
- Training Set: $X$
- $i^{th}$ (Input) Feature: $\bm{x_i}$
- $i^{th}$ (Output) Target: $\bm{y_i}$
- $m$: number of training examples
  - $(\bm{x^{(i)}}, \bm{y^{(i)}})$: $i^{th}$ training example
- $X_2^{(5)}$: 2nd feature of 5th training example

* Questions Before Building a ML Solution
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       977f1eda-b853-41e7-bc16-50b13ded041a
:END:
- What question am I trying to answer?
  - Do I think the data collected can answer that question?
- What is the best way to phrase my question as a machine learning problem?
- Have I collected *enough data* to represent the problem I want to solve?
- What *features* of the data did I extract, and will these enable the right predictions? [[id:e7223a85-4a76-47c2-94a3-b2d0dac4c2a4][Feature Engineering]]
- How will I *measure success* in my application?
  - [[id:e92991a2-8dd2-4816-82e3-66d09d410a78][Measuring Success]]
- How will the machine learning solution interact with other parts of my research or business product?
