#+title: Degrees of Freedom
#+filetags: :Statistics:

* Concept
- Dataset has 5 independent observations
- Four of the values are 2, 6, 8, and 5
- The sample mean is 5
- The last value is no longer independent, it must be 4

- Even though all five observations in the sample were independent,
  because we know an additional fact about the sample.
  - It has a mean of 5 -> we only have 4 degrees of freedom

* Degrees of Freedom
- Example1: You have to choose n numbers from all natural numbers.($DF=n$)
- Example2: You have n numbers that must sum to 10. ($DF=n-1$)
- Example3:
  [[../assets/Statistics/DegreesOfFreedom.png]]
  - $DF=4$

- Related to estimated population sd
  - $x_1+x_2+...+x_n=\bar{x}\cdot{n}$ (to get $\bar{x}\cdot{n}$ -> $DF=n-1$)
  - Only $n-1$ values are independent *after* we know the mean.

* Bessel's correction
- $\hat\sigma=\sqrt\frac{\sum_{i=1}^n(x_i-\bar{x})}{n-1}$
