* Highlights first synced by [[Readwise]] [[2023-03-11]]
** Strategized locking is the idea of the [Strategy Pattern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strategy_pattern) applied to locking. This means putting your locking strategy into an object and making it into a pluggable component of your system. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01gs6qtvtfw5j6eyvdvwdgzabs))
** two typical ways to implement strategized locking: run-time polymorphism (object orientation) or compile-time polymorphism (templates). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01gs6qvd35we26dn1dx1aeqjqj))
