* Jacobian Vector
- Partial derivatives: f respect to t
$$f(x, y)=\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\frac{dx}{dt} + \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\frac{dy}{dt}$$

- Given function: $f(x_1, x_2, x_3, ...)$

- The Jacobian vector is

$$J=[\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1},\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_2},\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_3},...]$$

* Jacobian Matrix

$$u(x,y)=x-2y$$

$$v(x,y)=3y-2x$$

$$J_u=\begin{bmatrix}\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\end{bmatrix}$$

$$J_v=\begin{bmatrix}\frac{\partial v}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial v}{\partial y}\end{bmatrix}$$

$$J=\begin{bmatrix}\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} \\
\frac{\partial v}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial v}{\partial y}\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}1 & -2 \\ -2 & 3\end{bmatrix}$$

The gradients are constant everywhere.
