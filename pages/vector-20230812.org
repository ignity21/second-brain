* Vector Laws

** Addition
- Commutative
- Associative
- Additive Identity: There exists an element $\bm{0}$ such that $\bm{a}+\bm{0}=\bm{a}$
- Additive Inverse: $\forall{\bm{a}}\exists{\bm{b}}\|\bm{a}+\bm{b}=\bm{0}$
- Geometric Translation :: Add a vector $\bm{v}$ to all other vectors, then the whole shape gets shifted by $\bm{v}$

** Scale Multiplication
- Commutative
- Associative
- Distributive: $s(\bm{a}+\bm{b})=s\bm{a}+s\bm{b}$

** Dot Product
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       cf7d5ccb-6bda-4d50-9a97-660f3de88709
:END:
*** Definition
\[\bm{x}\cdot\bm{y}=\sum_{i=1}^n x_i y_i\]

*** Geometric Meaning
- Cosine Rule: $c^2=a^2+b^2-2ab\cos{\theta}$
- $\bm{v}\cdot\bm{v}=\|\bm{v}\|^2=\sum_{i=1}v_i^2$
- $\|\bm{x}-\bm{y}\|^2=\|\bm{x}\|^2+\|\bm{y}\|^2-2\|\bm{x}\|\|\bm{y}\|\cos\theta$
  - where $\theta$ is the angle between $\bm{x}$ and $\bm{y}$.

\begin{align*}
\|\bm{x}-\bm{y}\|^2 & = (\bm{x}-\bm{y})\cdot(\bm{x}-\bm{y}) \\
& = \sum_{i=1}(x_i-y_i)(x_i-y_i) \\
& = \sum_{i=1}(x_i^2+y_i^2-2 x_i y_i) \\
& = \bm{x}\cdot\bm{x} + \bm{y}\cdot\bm{y} - 2\bm{x}\cdot\bm{y}
\end{align*}

- $\bm{x}\cdot\bm{y} = \|\bm{x}\|\|\bm{y}\|\cos\theta$
*** Code
#+begin_src python
  # dot product of x and y
  np.dot(x, y)
  x.dot(y)
  x @ y

  # element-wise product, not a dot product
  x * y
#+end_src

*** Properties
- Commutative: $\bm{u}\cdot\bm{v}=\bm{v}\cdot\bm{u}$
- NOT associative: $(\bm{u}\cdot\bm{v})\cdot \bm{w}$ is not defined.($\text{scalar }\cdot\bm{w}$)
- Distributive over Addition: $\bm{u}\cdot(\bm{v}+\bm{w})=\bm{u}\cdot\bm{v}+\bm{u}\cdot\bm{w}$
- Associative with regards to scalar multiplication
  - $\lambda\times(\bm{u}\cdot\bm{v})=(\lambda\times\bm{u})\cdot\bm{v}=\bm{u}\cdot(\lambda\times\bm{v})$

*** Calculating the angle between vectors
\[\theta=\arccos(\frac{\bm{x}\cdot\bm{y}}{\|\bm{x}\|\times\|\bm{y}\|})\]
#+begin_src python
  # in radians
  cos_theta = np.dot(x, y) / LA.norm(x) / LA.norm(y)
  theta = np.arccos(np.clip(cos_theta, -1, 1))
  # Due to small floating point errors, cos_theta may be very slightly outside of the [-1, 1]
  # Should clips the value first
#+end_src

* Norm(Magnitudes)
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       2963e2dc-7b96-47f1-aa18-26960815fc4a
:END:
\[\|\bm{u}\|=\sqrt{\sum_{i=1} u_i^2}\]
- The norm of $\bm{u}$ is the length of $\bm{u}$
- A *unit vector* is a vector with a norm equal to 1.
- *Normalized Vector* $\bm{\hat{u}}=\frac{\bm{u}}{\|\bm{u}\|}$
*** Code
#+begin_src python
  from numpy import linalg as LA
  LA.norm(u)
#+end_src

* Projection
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       a4782b65-f01d-48a9-8937-608fb2dd9de9
:END:
** Scalar Projection
- $\|\bm{y}\|\cos\theta$ is the scalar *projection of y on x*.
- $\text{Scale Projection }=\frac{\bm{x}\cdot\bm{y}}{\|\bm{x}\|}$
- \(\text{Dot product } = (\text{scalar projection } \bm{y} \text{ on } \bm{x}) \times (\text{length of } \bm{x})\)
- When the vectors are Perpendicular, meaning dot product is 0. (scalar projection is 0)

** Vector Projection
- Scalar Projection is the length of the projection *y* on *x*
- Vector Projection $\bm{proj_x y}$
  - $proj_x y=\text{scalar projection}\times\frac{\bm{x}}{\|\bm{x}\|}=\frac{\bm{y}\cdot\bm{x}}{\|\bm{x}\|}\times\frac{\bm{x}}{\|\bm{x}\|}=\frac{\bm{y}\cdot\bm{x}}{\bm{x}\cdot\bm{x}}\cdot \bm{x}$
  - $proj_x y=(\bm{y}\cdot\bm{\hat{x}})\times\bm{\hat{x}}$
  - $\frac{\bm{x}}{\|\bm{x}\|}$ or $\bm{\hat{x}}$ is the unit vector of *x* (aka normalized vector)
  - The dot product is also very useful to project points onto an axis
#+begin_src python
  u_normalized = u / LA.norm(u)
  proj = v.dot(u_normalized) * u_normalized
#+end_src
