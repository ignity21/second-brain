** The 6 classic options trading books everyone needs on their bookshelf: 

![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/Fp2yKzBXgAEnYB2.jpg) ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/pyquantnews/status/1629654544257892353))
** The Volatility Surface by Jim Gatheral

What you learn:

How to create a volatility surface with different volatility models (Herston, SVJ, SABR) and use it for pricing options. 

![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/Fp2yZNpWIAApvMK.jpg) ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/pyquantnews/status/1629654789222006792))
** Option Volatility & Pricing by Sheldon Natenberg

What you learn:

How to price options, manage risk, and trade advanced options strategies. 

![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/Fp2yokJXoAAGmZD.jpg) ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/pyquantnews/status/1629655053416947712))
** Options as a Strategic Investment by Lawrence G. McMillan

What you learn:

Dozens of different complex options positions and when to trade them. This is the Bible of options trading strategies. 

![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/Fp2y3o1XwAAn7dX.jpg) ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/pyquantnews/status/1629655312830529536))
** Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives by John C. Hull

What you learn:

Derivatives and volatility pricing for a wide range of assets. Literally the textbook for derivatives pricing, 

![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/Fp2zG_7XsAAs_p5.jpg) ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/pyquantnews/status/1629655575691767808))
** Volatility Trading by Euan Sinclair

What you learn:

How to measure statistical volatility to more accurately price options. 

![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/Fp2zUgOWcAIPAtx.jpg) ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/pyquantnews/status/1629655808207142912))
** Financial Calculus by Martin Baxter and Andrew Rennie

What you learn:

The math behind pricing, construction, and hedging of derivatives. 

![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/Fp2zjKuWcAANQW6.jpg) ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/pyquantnews/status/1629656061199130629))
