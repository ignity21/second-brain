* Basis Transformation
- $B$ is the new basis written in old basis language.
- $B^{-1}$ is the old basis written in new basis language.

\[B\cdot\begin{bmatrix} x_{new} \\ y_{new} \end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix} x_{old} \\ y_{old} \end{bmatrix}\]

\[B^{-1}\cdot \begin{bmatrix} x_{old} \\ y_{old} \end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix} x_{new} \\ y_{new} \end{bmatrix}\]

* Constructing an Orthonormal Basis
- The *Gram–Schmidt* process
- Given an linearly independent vector set V

  \[V=\{v_1, v_2, \cdots, v_n\}\]
*** $e_1$
    \[e_1=\frac{v_1}{|v_1|}\]

*** $e_2$
    \[v_2=\frac{v_2\cdot e_1}{|e_1|}\cdot\frac{e_1}{|e_1|}+u_2\]
    - $u_2$ is perpendicular to $e_1$

    \[u_2=v_2-(v_2\cdot e_1)\times e_1\]

    \[e_2=\frac{u_2}{|u_2|}\]

*** $e_3$
    \[u_3=v_3-(v_3\cdot e_1)\times e_1-(v_3\cdot e_2)\times e_2\]
    - $u_3$ is perpendicular to the plane($e_1$, $e_2$)

    \[e_3=\frac{u_3}{|u_3|}\]
