* Concept
** Parameters
- $\bm{w}$: $w_1, w_2, \cdots, w_n$
- $b$

** Model
\[f_{\bm{w}, b}(\bm{x})= w_1 x_1 + w_2 x_2 + \cdots + w_n x_n + b=\bm{w}^T\cdot\bm{x} + b\]
- ~f = np.dot(w, x) + b~

** Cost Function
\[J(\bm{w}, b)=\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^m Cost(f_{\bm{w}, b}(\bm{x^{(i)}}), \bm{y^{(i)}})\]
- Squared Error Function(or "Mean squared error")
- The mean is halved $\frac{1}{2}$ as a convenience for the computation of the gradient descent
\[Cost(f_{\bm{w},b}(\bm{x^{(i)}}), \bm{y^{(i)}}))=\frac{1}{2}(f_{\bm{w},b}(\bm{x^{(i)}})-\bm{y^{(i)}})^2\]

\[J(\bm{w}, b)=\frac{1}{2m}\sum_{i=1}^m(\bm{w}^T\bm{x^{(i)}}+b-\bm{y^{(i)}})^2\]

** Goal: Minimize $J(\bm{w}, b)$
*** Gradient Descent Algorithm
- $\alpha$: Learning rate
- repeat until convergence (no longer change much with each additional step)
- Simultaneously update $w_1$ to $w_j$ and $b$
\[w_j=w_j-\alpha\frac{\partial}{\partial w_j}J(\bm{w}, b)\]

\[b=b-\alpha\frac{\partial}{\partial b}J(\bm{w}, b)\]

**** Details
\[\frac{\partial{J(\bm{w}, b)}}{\partial{w_j}}=\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m} (f_{\bm{w}, b}(\bm{x}^{(i)}) -\bm{y^{(i)}})x_{j}^{(i)}\]

\[\frac{\partial{J(\bm{w}, b)}}{\partial{b}}=\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m} (f_{\bm{w}, b}(\bm{x}^{(i)}) -\bm{y^{(i)}})\]


\[w_j=w_j-\alpha\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m} (\bm{w}^T\bm{x^{(i)}}+b-\bm{y^{(i)}})x_{j}^{(i)}\]

\[b=b-\alpha\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m} (\bm{w}^T\bm{x^{(i)}}+b-\bm{y^{(i)}})\]

* Vectorized Summary $\theta_0$ and $x_0=1$ Version
** Parameters
\[\bm{\theta}=\begin{bmatrix}
\theta_0 \\ \theta_1 \\ \vdots \\ \theta_n
\end{bmatrix}\]

\[\bm{\theta}^T=\begin{bmatrix}
\theta_0 &
\theta_1 & \cdots &
\theta_n
\end{bmatrix}\]

** Model
\[\bm{X}=\begin{bmatrix}
x_0^{(1)}\bm{(1)} & x_1^{(1)} & x_2^{(1)} & \cdots & x_n^{(1)} \\
x_0^{(2)}\bm{(1)} & x_1^{(2)} & x_2^{(2)} & \cdots & x_n^{(2)} \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots  \\
x_0^{(m)}\bm{(1)} & x_1^{(m)} & x_2^{(m)} & \cdots & x_n^{(m)}
\end{bmatrix}\]

\[f(\bm{x})=\bm{\theta}^T\bm{x}\]

\[f_{\bm{\theta}}(\bm{X})=\bm{X}\bm{\theta}\]
- $f_{\bm{\theta}}(\bm{X})$ is a vector of hypotheses

** Cost Function
\[J(\bm{\theta})=\frac{1}{2m}\sum_{i=1}^{m}(\bm{\theta}^T \bm{x^{(i)}}-\bm{y}^{(i)})^2\]
- Vectorized

\[J(\bm{\theta})=\frac{1}{2m}(\bm{X}\bm{\theta}-\bm{y})^T(\bm{X}\bm{\theta}-\bm{y})\]

Python code:
#+begin_src python
  J = (1/2*m) * ((X @ theta - y) ** 2).sum()
#+end_src

** Gradient Descent
- repeat until convergence
\[\bm{\theta}=\bm{\theta}-\alpha\frac{1}{m}\mathbf{X}^T(\mathbf{X}\bm{\theta}-\bm{y})\]

* TODO Regularized Linear Regression
** Cost Function
\[J(\bm{w})=\frac{1}{2m}\sum_{i=1}^m (f_{\bm{w},b}{(x_i)}-{y_i})^2+\lambda\sum_{j=1}^nw_j^2\]
- $\lambda$ is the regularization parameter. It determines how much the costs of our theta parameters are inflated.
- $w_0$ should not be penalized

** Gradient Descent
\[w_0=w_0-\alpha\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m} (w^T\bm{x_i}-y_i)x_{i0}\]

\[w_j=w_j-\alpha[\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m} (w^T\bm{x_i}-y_i)x_{ij}+\frac{\lambda}{m}w_j]\]
- can also be represented as:

\[w_j=w_j(1-\alpha\frac{\lambda}{m})-\frac{\alpha}{m}\sum_{i=1}^m(f_{\bm{w},b}(x_i)-y_i)x_{ij}\]

** Normal Equation
\[w=(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X}+\lambda\cdot \mathbf{L})^{-1}\mathbf{X}^T\bm{y}\]
- $L$ is a matrix with 0 at the top left and 1's down the diagonal, with 0's everywhere else.
