#+title: Regularized Linear Regression
#+filetags: ML

* Cost Function
- For mean squared error loss function
- The hyperparameter $\lambda$ controls how much you want to regularize the model.

\[J(\bm{w}, b) = \frac{1}{2m}\sum_{i=1}^m(f_{\bm{w}, b}(\bm{x^{(i)}})-y^{(i)})^2+\frac{\lambda}{2m}\sum_{j=1}^n w_j^2\]

* Gradient Descent
- Repeat
\[w_j=w_j-\alpha\frac{\partial}{\partial w_j}J(\bm{w}, b)\]

\[b=b-\alpha\frac{\partial}{\partial b}J(\bm{w}, b)\]

** Derivative Terms
\[\frac{\partial{J(\bm{w}, b)}}{\partial{w_j}}=\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m} (f_{\bm{w}, b}(\bm{x^{(i)}}) -y^{(i)})x_{j}^{(i)}+\frac{\lambda}{m}w_j\]

\[\frac{\partial{J(\bm{w}, b)}}{\partial{b}}=\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m} (f_{\bm{w}, b}(\bm{x^{(i)}}) -y^{(i)})\]

** Details
\begin{align*}
w_j & = w_j-\alpha\frac{\partial}{\partial w_j}J(\bm{w}, b) \\
    & = w_j-\alpha[\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m} (f_{\bm{w}, b}(\bm{x^{(i)}}) -y^{(i)})x_{j}^{(i)}+\frac{\lambda}{m}w_j] \\
    & = w_j(1-\alpha\frac{\lambda}{m})-\alpha\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m} (f_{\bm{w}, b}(\bm{x^{(i)}}) -y^{(i)})x_{j}^{(i)}
\end{align*}
- Term \((1-\alpha\frac{\lambda}{m})\) slightly less than 1 and shrinks \(w_j\) a little bit.
