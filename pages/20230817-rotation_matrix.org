#+title: Rotation Matrix
#+filetags: :LinearAlgebra:Math:

* 2D

\[\begin{bmatrix}
\cos\theta & \sin\theta \\
-\sin\theta & \cos\theta
\end{bmatrix}\]

* 3D

\[\begin{bmatrix}
\cos\theta & \sin\theta & 0 \\
-\sin\theta & \cos\theta & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}\]
