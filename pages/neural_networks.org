* Notations
- \(\bm{w_j^{(l)}}, b_i^{(l)}\): the parameters of /jth/ unit/activation in layer /l/
- \(\bm{a^{[l]}}\): the outputs of layer /l/
  - A 3-layer example: \(\bm{x}(\bm{a^{[0]}})\Longrightarrow\bm{a^{[1]}}\Longrightarrow\bm{a^{[2]}}\Longrightarrow y(a^{[3]})\)
  - \(a_j^{[l]}\): the /jth/ output of layer /l/

* Activation Value
#+CAPTION: Layer Computation
[[../assets/ML/NNLayerComputation.png]]
- Activation Function(sigmoid): \(a_j^{[l]}=g(\bm{w_j^{[l]}}\cdot\bm{a^{[l-1]}} + b_j^{[l]})\)
  - example: \(a_2^{[3]}=g(\bm{w_2^{[3]}}\cdot\bm{a^{[2]}}+b_2^{[3]})\)
