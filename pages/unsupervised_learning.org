#+TITLE: Unsupervised Learning
#+filetags: :ML:

- tags :: [[id:9d343f34-3ee6-4737-8612-6718f77dba03][Machine Learning]]


Unsupervised learning allows us to approach problems with little or no idea
what our results should look like.
- With unsupervised learning there is *no feedback* based on the prediction results.

* Examples
- [[id:b19d3b93-0200-4d6b-8c68-10acd92b2fcd][Clustering]]: Take a collection of 1,000,000 different genes, and find a way to
  automatically group these genes into groups that are somehow similar or related
  by different variables, such as lifespan, location, roles, and so on.
- [[id:299fced8-3be5-47ef-96d2-a5fa2fa043a1][Non-Clustering]]: The "Cocktail Party Algorithm", allows you to find structure in a
  chaotic environment. (i.e. identifying individual voices and music from a mesh of
  sounds at a cocktail party).
