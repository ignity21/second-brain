#+title: Types of Data
#+filetags: :DataScience:Math:Statistics:

* Numeric
** Continuous (Measured)
- Airplane Speed
- Time spent waiting in line

** Discrete (Counted)
- Number of pets
- Number of packages shipped

* Categorical
** Nominal (Unordered)
- Married/unmarried
- Country of residence

** Ordinal (Ordered)
- Strongly disagree
- Somewhat disagree
- Neither agree nor disagree
- Somewhat agree
- Strongly agree

** Categorical in Python
- Nominal
  - ~df['str_col'].astype('category')~
  - ~pd.Series(my_list, dtype='category')~
  - ~pd.read_csv('data.csv', dtype={'Status': 'category'})~
- Ordinal
  - ~pd.Categorical(my_list, categories=['C', 'B', 'A'], ordered=True)~
- Category type saves memory: ~series.nbytes~
