#+title: Monetary Policy
#+filetags: :Trading:

* Definition
Monetary policy is the process of:
- Supplying currency
  - less currency slower economy
- Setting interest rates
  - lower interest rates -> stronger economy

* In US
FOMC (Federal Open Market Committee)
- 8 meetings per year
- Decision on appropriate monetary policy
- Setting up rules for banks
- Setting interest rates
