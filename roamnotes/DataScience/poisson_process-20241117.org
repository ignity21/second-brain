#+title: Poisson Process
#+filetags: :Statistics:Probability:Math:

* Poisson process
Events appear to happen at a certain rate, but *completely at random*
- Number of animals adopted from an animal shelter *per week*
  - Event: one animal is adopted
- Number of people arriving at a restaurant *per hour*
- Number of earthquakes in US *per year*

* \(\lambda\)
- Average rate of events per time period
- Example: if on average 3 animals are adopted per week, then \(\lambda = 3\) adoptions/week
