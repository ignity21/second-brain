#+title: IQR


- IQR is an *absolute measure* of dispersion

* Quartile Coefficient of Dispersion
- A version of the IQR that is normalized.
  - Formula: \[QCD = \frac{Q_3 - Q_1}{Q_3 + Q_1}\]
- Benefit: You can now compare the spread of "elephant weights" and "ant weights" fairly, as the units cancel out.
- In many normalization formulas, you divide by the average to see how large the variation is compared to the size of the numbers.
  - \[Q_3 - Q_1\] term acts as a *measure of spread*. \[\frac{Q_3 - Q_1}{2}\] is the semi-interquartile range.
  - \[Q_3 + Q_1\] acts as a "Relative" Average \[\frac{Q_3 + Q_1}{2}\]
  - So the QCD is a ratio of *Semi-IQR* to *Mean of Quartiles(Midhinge)*.
