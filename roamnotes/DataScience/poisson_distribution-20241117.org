#+title: Poisson Distribution
#+filetags: :Probability:Math:Statistics:
* [[id:85ef2d1a-c912-46c7-a4b1-3af274c4fd4c][Poisson Process]]
- \(\lambda\) = average number of events per time interval
* Definition
- Poisson distribution: Probability of some # of events occurring over a fixed period of time.
  - Probability of 12 people arriving at a restaurant per hour (PMF)
  - Probability of > 5 animals adopted from an animal shelter per week (1 - CDF)

* Python Examples
** Generating Data
- ~poisson.rvs(lambda, size=10)~

** Calculating PMF & CDF
#+begin_src python
from scipy.stats import poisson

# What is P(# of customers in a hour = 2)? if the average # of customers per hour is 5
prob = poisson.pmf(2, 5)

# What is P(# of customers in a hour <= 3)? if the average # of customers per hour is 5
prob = poisson.cdf(3, 5)
#+end_src
