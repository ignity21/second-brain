#+title: Normalizer
#+filetags: :ML:

* Overview
Normalizer is used to scale individual samples to have unit norm.
- it adjusts the values of each sample vector (row) such that the vector has a norm (magnitude) of 1.
- useful when the direction of the vector is more important than its magnitude such as in text classification or clustering.

* Methods
- L1 Normalization :: Scales the vector such that the sum of the absolute values is 1. This is useful when you want to emphasize sparsity.

- L2 Normalization :: Scales the vector such that the sum of the squares is 1. This is the most common normalization method, as it maintains the Euclidean distance between points.

- Max Normalization :: Scales the vector such that the maximum absolute value is 1. This method is useful when you want to emphasize the strongest signal in the sample.
