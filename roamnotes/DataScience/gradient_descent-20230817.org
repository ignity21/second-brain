* Gradient Descent
- [[id:da701a4c-988e-427b-bf36-33cbe7a470c2][Learning Rate]]: $\alpha$
- Simultaneously update $w_1$ to $w_j$ and $b$
\[w_j=w_j-\alpha\frac{\partial}{\partial w_j}J(\bm{w}, b)\]

\[b=b-\alpha\frac{\partial}{\partial b}J(\bm{w}, b)\]

* [[id:7ac9ccb4-374e-4c58-b932-7704b3c86381][Regularized Linear Regression]]
** Gradient Update
$$\theta_0=\theta_0-\frac{\alpha}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n(h_\theta(x^{(i)})-y^{(i)})$$

$$\theta_j=\theta_j-\frac{\alpha}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n(h_\theta(x^{(i)})-y^{(i)})x_j^{(i)}-\lambda\theta_j$$

- rewrite as:
$$\theta_j=\theta_j(1-\alpha\lambda)-\frac{\alpha}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n(h_\theta(x^{(i)})-y^{(i)})x_j^{(i)}$$
