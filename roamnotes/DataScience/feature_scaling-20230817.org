* Methods
** Min-Max Scaling
\[x_{scaled}^{(j)}=\frac{x^{(j)}-min(\bm{x})}{max(\bm{x})-min(\bm{x})}\]

** Mean Normalization
\[x_{scaled}^{(j)}=\frac{x^{(j)}-\mu_x}{max(\bm{x})-min(\bm{x})}\]

** Z-score Normalization
\[x_{scaled}^{(j)}=\frac{x^{(j)}-\mu_x}{\sigma_x}\]
