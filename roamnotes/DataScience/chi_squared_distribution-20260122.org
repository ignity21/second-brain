#+title: Chi-squared Distribution
#+filetags: :Statistics:

* Overview
- Alias: \(X^2\)
- Continuous probability distribution
- We use it to measure *discrepancy*
  - discrepancy: how much our observed data deviates from what we expected
- The \(X^2\) distribution is directly derived from the *Standard Normal Distribution* =Z~N(0,1)=.

* When is it used?
- Goodness of Fit :: Does your data follow a specific distribution? Calculating the *Chi-square statistic*
- Test of Independence :: Are two categorical variables related? For example, "Does gender affect voting preference?"
- Inferences about Variance :: If you want to test if a population's variance $\sigma^2$ is equal to a specific value, the sample variance $s^2$ (when scaled) follows a $\chi^2$ distribution.
