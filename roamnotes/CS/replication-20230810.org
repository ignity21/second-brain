#+title: Replication
#+filetags: SystemDesign

- links :: [[id:28a88260-5abb-48a9-bf27-5678acd7be3b][Reliability]]

The act of duplicating the data from one database server to others.

- to increase the redundancy of your system and tolerate regional failures for instance.
- to move data closer to your clients, thus decreasing the latency.
