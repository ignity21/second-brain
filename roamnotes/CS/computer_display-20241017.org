#+title: Computer Display
#+filetags: :Hardware:

* LCD (Liquid Crystal Display)
- LCDs require backlighting, often provided by LEDs
  - The backlighting unit (BLU) requires extra space

** Common LCD Types
- In-Plane Switching (IPS)
- Twisted Nematic (TN)
- VA-Vertical Alignment

* OLED (Organic Light Emitting Diodes)
OLEDs are diodes that emit light using organic (carbon-based) materials when electricity is passed through the diodes.

** Common OLED Types
- Active Matrix Organic Light Emitting Diode (AMOLED)
- Inorganic mini-LEDs (mLEDs)
- Inorganic micro-LEDs (μLEDs)
