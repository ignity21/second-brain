#+title: SSL Certificate
#+filetags: Network

A digital certificate granted to a server by a *certificate authority*.
- contains the server's public key
- are a crucial defense against *man-in-the-middle attack*

* CA Certificate Application
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       e6f70860-1c15-4c6f-90d7-3776a0e180e9
:END:
** Create a certificate signing request (CSR)
#+begin_src bash
  openssl req -new -newkey rsa:2048 -nodes -keyout example.com.key -out example.com.csr
#+end_src
- =example.com.key=: contains the private key for your certificate.
- =example.com.csr=: contains the information that you will submit to the CA.

** Submit the CSR to the CA

** CA generate your certificate
- =example.com.crt=: contains your certificate.
