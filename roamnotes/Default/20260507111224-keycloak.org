* Concepts
| Concept          | Analogy                     | Purpose                                 |
|------------------+-----------------------------+-----------------------------------------|
| Realm            | Isolated tenant / namespace | Separates the entire user space         |
| Group / Subgroup | Department / Team           | Manages users in bulk by membership     |
| Role Mapping     | Permission label            | Determines what a user is allowed to do |
