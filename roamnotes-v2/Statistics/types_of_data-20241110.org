#+title: Types of Data
#+filetags: :DataScience:Math:Statistics:

* Numeric
** Continuous (Measured)
- Airplane Speed
- Time spent waiting in line

** Discrete (Counted)
- Number of pets
- Number of packages shipped

* Categorical
** Nominal (Unordered)
- Married/unmarried
- Country of residence

** Ordinal (Ordered)
- Strongly disagree
- Somewhat disagree
- Neither agree nor disagree
- Somewhat agree
- Strongly agree
