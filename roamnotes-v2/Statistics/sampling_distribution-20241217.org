#+title: Sampling Distribution
#+filetags: :Python:DataScience:Statistics:

* Overview
- Sampling Distribution :: Distribution of sample *statistic of interest* (mean here)
| $X_i$                | Variable in Sample i        |
| $\bar{X_i}$          | sample mean                 |
| $n$                  | number of samples           |
| $M$                  | mean of sample means        |
| $SE$(Standard Error) | standard deviation of means |

- $M=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\bar{X_i}$
- Ratio of $\sigma$ to $SE$: $\frac{\sigma}{SE}=\sqrt{n}$
  - [[id:193270c8-6738-48fb-b728-8462f10de3c5][Central Limit Theorem]]
