#+title: Rotation Matrix
#+filetags: :LinearAlgebra:Math:

* 2D
This form rotates a column vector *clockwise* by $\theta$ (it maps $(1, 0)$ to $(\cos\theta, -\sin\theta)$); the counterclockwise rotation is its transpose, $\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta\end{bmatrix}$. The 3D matrix below rotates about the $z$ axis in the same direction.

\[\begin{bmatrix}
\cos\theta & \sin\theta \\
-\sin\theta & \cos\theta
\end{bmatrix}\]

* 3D

\[\begin{bmatrix}
\cos\theta & \sin\theta & 0 \\
-\sin\theta & \cos\theta & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}\]
