#+title: Omarchy Learning Path
#+filetags: :learning:Linux:Tools:

Started on 2026-11-10. The goal is to become comfortable with the Omarchy desktop and development environment: arranging windows, locating configuration, recovering from mistakes, and understanding who manages development tool versions. Target: awareness (see learning-goals), with muscle memory for common window shortcuts and lookup skills for the rest.

Many steps require practice at a computer. On a phone, read the notes and think through the core questions, then practice later. “To add” marks missing material to fill in when needed.
