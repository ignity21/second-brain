#+title: Statistics Learning Path
#+filetags: :learning:Statistics:

Started on 2026-11-10. The central question: Given a single sample, what can we say about the population, and how confident can we be? The first two stages establish the language of data and randomness. The third connects samples to populations; the next two cover interval estimation and hypothesis testing.

Each step has one core question. Move on when you can answer it in your own words. “To fix” records issues found in the notes while planning the path; address them when reaching that step.
