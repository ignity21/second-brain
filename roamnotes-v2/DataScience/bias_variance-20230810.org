#+title: Bias/Variance
#+filetags: ML

- Establishing a baseline level of performance.
  - e.g. Human level performance, or just 0

- $Bias = J_{train}-J_{base}$
- $Variance = j_{cv}-J_{train}$

* Problems
- High Bias: Underfitting
- High Variance: Overfitting
- High Bias and High variance
  - Some parts are overfitting, some are underfitting
