#+title: Text Functions

* Regions and the Kill Ring
- ~(zap-to-char (NTH CHAR))~
- ~(kill-region)~ It clips text from a region and copies that text to the kill ring.
- ~(copy-region-as-kill)~: copies a region of text from a buffer and saves it in the kill-ring.

* Example: ~zap-to-char~
#+begin_src elisp
(defun zap-to-char (arg char)
  "Kill through ARGth occurrence of CHAR."
  (interactive "p\ncZap to char: ")
  (kill-region
   (point)
   (progn
     (search-forward (char-to-string char) nil nil arg)
     (point))))
#+end_src
